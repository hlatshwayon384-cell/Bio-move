Biomechanical Joint-Assistance System for Lunar Analogue Operations

LIVE TELEMETRY
Operational Hazard Mitigation

Active Lower-Limb Assistance for Extravehicular Activity

In pressurized lunar suit environments, continuous counter-pressure and extra suit mass drastically increase metabolic expenditure and fatigue at the knee and ankle joints. BIO-MOVE integrates lightweight variable torque actuators and real-time kinematic strain monitoring to stabilize bio-joint load during analogue lunar traverses.
